Awesome Tattoo Design

A curated list of tools and references for planning and designing a tattoo: design services, stencils, lettering, flower meanings, placement, safety, finding an artist and history.

Design tools and services

  • LastingLine — Custom designs for birth flowers, names and memorials: pay once for three versions, names spelled exactly as typed, a stencil outline and a real-size print sheet for your artist. (paid)
  • TattoosAI — AI tattoo generator that draws a design from a written description. (freemium)
  • Fotor AI Tattoo Generator — AI tattoo generator in the browser, with a preview on the body. (freemium)
  • Photoleap AI Tattoo Generator — AI tattoo generator in the Photoleap app, including ambigrams. (freemium)
  • Tat.ink — AI tattoo design platform for creating and exploring custom designs. (freemium)
  • Custom Tattoo Design — Custom tattoo designs drawn by a team of designers. (paid)
  • Tattfly Birth Flower Tattoo Generator — Birth flower tattoo generator for single stems or family bouquets, with no sign-up.
  • AI for Tattoo — AI tattoo generator with a virtual try-on and a free plan. (freemium)

Drawing apps

  • Procreate — iPad drawing and animation app, paid once with no subscription. (paid)
  • Clip Studio Paint — Drawing app for illustration, comics and animation on several platforms. (paid)

Stencils

  • LastingLine Tattoo Stencil Maker — Turns a design or drawing into a stencil outline in the browser; the image never leaves your device.
  • FreeStencilMaker — Turns a photo into a multi-layer stencil; exports SVG, PDF or DXF.

Lettering

  • Tattoo Font Generator — Previews your text in tattoo lettering styles, sizes and fonts.
  • Font Meme Tattoo Fonts — A catalogue of tattoo fonts with a text preview.
  • Cinzel — Serif typeface inspired by first-century Roman inscriptions (Google Fonts).
  • Cormorant Garamond — Garamond-style display family by Christian Thalmann (Google Fonts).
  • Great Vibes — Flowing script with casual capitals and formal lowercase (Google Fonts).
  • Alex Brush — Brush script with short ascenders and descenders (Google Fonts).
  • Pirata One — Condensed gothic textura (Google Fonts).
  • UnifrakturMaguntia — Fraktur based on a 1901 typeface by Carl Albert Fahrenwaldt (Google Fonts).
  • The League of Moveable Type — Open-source type foundry.

Flower meanings and symbols

  • Language of Flowers — Kate Greenaway (1884) — The Victorian flower-meaning book as a free Project Gutenberg ebook.
  • Plant Lore, Legends, and Lyrics — Richard Folkard (1884) — Plant myths and folklore, scanned on the Internet Archive.
  • Flora Historica — Henry Phillips — A 19th-century history of garden flowers, scanned on the Internet Archive.
  • Symbols, Signs & Signets — Ernst Lehner — An illustrated book of historic symbols and emblems on the Internet Archive.
  • USDA PLANTS Database — Names and ranges of North American plants, to check which flower is which.
  • RHS Plants — The Royal Horticultural Society's plant pages.

Placement

  • Anatomy 3D Atlas — 3D human anatomy with muscle layers, for thinking about how a design sits on the body. (freemium)
  • Z-Anatomy — Open-source 3D anatomy atlas.

Safety and aftercare

  • FDA — Think Before You Ink: Tattoo Safety — The U.S. FDA's consumer page on tattoo safety.
  • American Academy of Dermatology — Tattoos and piercings — Dermatologists' advice on caring for tattooed skin.
  • Better Health Channel — Tattoos — The Victorian (Australia) government's health page on getting a tattoo.
  • Alliance of Professional Tattooists — Resources — Resources from a professional tattooists' association.

Finding an artist

  • Tattoodo — Browse tattoo ideas and book an artist near you. (freemium)
  • Tattoo Filter — Tattoo gallery and artist, studio and event directory.
  • National Tattoo Association — A U.S. tattoo association's news and articles.

Learning to design tattoos

  • Reinventing the Tattoo — Education for tattooers and apprentices. (paid)
  • 10 Masters — Online tattoo courses taught by professionals (in Spanish). (paid)
  • Proko — Figure and portrait drawing lessons. (freemium)
  • Drawabox — A free drawing fundamentals course.

History and archives

  • The Tattoo Archive — Hundreds of articles on tattoo history.
  • Lyle Tuttle Tattoo Collection — The tattoo collection of the tattooist Lyle Tuttle.
  • Wikimedia Commons — Tattoo designs — Openly licensed images of tattoo designs.
