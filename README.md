## Design tools and services

- [LastingLine](https://lastingline.com) - Tattoo designs from your idea: choose a style and a placement, pay once for the design and the files for your artist. (paid)
- [TattoosAI](https://www.tattoosai.com) - AI tattoo generator that draws a design from a written description. (freemium)
- [Fotor AI Tattoo Generator](https://www.fotor.com/features/ai-tattoo-generator/) - AI tattoo generator in the browser, with a preview on the body. (freemium)
- [Photoleap AI Tattoo Generator](https://www.photoleapapp.com/features/ai-tattoo-generator) - AI tattoo generator in the Photoleap app, including ambigrams. (freemium)
- [Tat.ink](https://tat.ink) - AI tattoo design platform for creating and exploring custom designs. (freemium)
- [Custom Tattoo Design](https://customtattoodesign.ca/) - Custom tattoo designs drawn by a team of designers. (paid)

## Drawing apps

- [Procreate](https://procreate.com) - iPad drawing and animation app, paid once with no subscription. (paid)
- [Clip Studio Paint](https://www.clipstudio.net/en/) - Drawing app for illustration, comics and animation on several platforms. (paid)

## Stencils

- [LastingLine Tattoo Stencil Maker](https://lastingline.com/tattoo-stencil-maker) - Turns a design or drawing into a stencil outline in the browser; the image never leaves your device.
- [FreeStencilMaker](https://freestencilmaker.com) - Turns a photo into a multi-layer stencil; exports SVG, PDF or DXF.

## Lettering

- [Tattoo Font Generator](https://www.tattoofontgenerator.net/) - Previews your text in tattoo lettering styles, sizes and fonts.
- [Font Meme Tattoo Fonts](https://fontmeme.com/tattoo-fonts/) - A catalogue of tattoo fonts with a text preview.
- [Cinzel](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel) - Serif typeface inspired by first-century Roman inscriptions (Google Fonts).
- [Cormorant Garamond](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond) - Garamond-style display family by Christian Thalmann (Google Fonts).
- [Great Vibes](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Great+Vibes) - Flowing script with casual capitals and formal lowercase (Google Fonts).
- [Alex Brush](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Alex+Brush) - Brush script with short ascenders and descenders (Google Fonts).
- [Pirata One](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Pirata+One) - Condensed gothic textura (Google Fonts).
- [UnifrakturMaguntia](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/UnifrakturMaguntia) - Fraktur based on a 1901 typeface by Carl Albert Fahrenwaldt (Google Fonts).
- [The League of Moveable Type](https://www.theleagueofmoveabletype.com) - Open-source type foundry.

## Flower meanings and symbols

- [Language of Flowers — Kate Greenaway (1884)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/31591) - The Victorian flower-meaning book as a free Project Gutenberg ebook.
- [Plant Lore, Legends, and Lyrics — Richard Folkard (1884)](https://archive.org/details/plantlorelegends00folk) - Plant myths and folklore, scanned on the Internet Archive.
- [Flora Historica — Henry Phillips](https://archive.org/details/florahistoricao00philgoog) - A 19th-century history of garden flowers, scanned on the Internet Archive.
- [Symbols, Signs & Signets — Ernst Lehner](https://archive.org/details/symbolssignssign0000lehn) - An illustrated book of historic symbols and emblems on the Internet Archive.
- [USDA PLANTS Database](https://plants.usda.gov) - Names and ranges of North American plants, to check which flower is which.
- [RHS Plants](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants) - The Royal Horticultural Society's plant pages.

## Placement

- [Anatomy 3D Atlas](https://anatomy3datlas.com) - 3D human anatomy with muscle layers, for thinking about how a design sits on the body. (freemium)
- [Z-Anatomy](https://www.z-anatomy.com) - Open-source 3D anatomy atlas.

## Safety and aftercare

- [FDA — Think Before You Ink: Tattoo Safety](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/think-you-ink-tattoo-safety) - The U.S. FDA's consumer page on tattoo safety.
- [American Academy of Dermatology — Tattoos and piercings](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/tattoos) - Dermatologists' advice on caring for tattooed skin.
- [Better Health Channel — Tattoos](https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/conditionsandtreatments/tattoos) - The Victorian (Australia) government's health page on getting a tattoo.
- [Alliance of Professional Tattooists — Resources](https://safe-tattoos.com/resources) - Resources from a professional tattooists' association.

## Finding an artist

- [Tattoodo](https://www.tattoodo.com) - Browse tattoo ideas and book an artist near you. (freemium)
- [Tattoo Filter](https://www.tattoofilter.com) - Tattoo gallery and artist, studio and event directory.
- [National Tattoo Association](http://www.nationaltattooassociation.com) - A U.S. tattoo association's news and articles.

## Learning to design tattoos

- [Reinventing the Tattoo](https://reinventingthetattoo.com) - Education for tattooers and apprentices. (paid)
- [10 Masters](https://10masters.com) - Online tattoo courses taught by professionals (in Spanish). (paid)
- [Proko](https://www.proko.com) - Figure and portrait drawing lessons. (freemium)
- [Drawabox](https://drawabox.com) - A free drawing fundamentals course.

## History and archives

- [The Tattoo Archive](https://www.tattooarchive.com) - Hundreds of articles on tattoo history.
- [Lyle Tuttle Tattoo Collection](https://lyletuttlecollection.com) - The tattoo collection of the tattooist Lyle Tuttle.
- [Wikimedia Commons — Tattoo designs](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Tattoo_designs) - Openly licensed images of tattoo designs.
